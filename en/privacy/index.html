Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

In short: we only process what an analysis needs. Your clip has its audio removed, is analysed, then deleted immediately or within 30 days depending on your setting. We do not sell data. You can delete your account in the app at any time.

Controller

Erdem Tekin, erdemtekin1995@gmail.com.

Data we process

  • Account: email; name and user ID when you use Sign in with Apple or Google; a random ID for guest accounts. Kept until you delete your account, then permanently erased within 30 days.
  • Video clips (audio removed): used only for the analysis. Deleted right after the analysis if "Delete video right after analysis" is on, otherwise after 30 days. Rejected and failed clips are deleted at once. Kept 90 days only if you explicitly allow it when sending feedback.
  • Context and reports: optional details you add (question, moment, shirt colour), the generated report and a thumbnail. Kept until you delete the analysis or your account. The referee decision you enter is never sent to the AI.
  • Votes and feedback, push token, pseudonymous usage events and crash logs (Sentry, PostHog): used to improve accuracy and fix bugs. Usage data is kept for up to 12 months.

Processors

The clip goes to Google Gemini API (USA), which describes what happens in it. Anthropic Claude API (USA) never sees the video; it receives only that description and the rule text, and writes the rule interpretation. Neither provider uses API data to train its models. App data is hosted in the EU: database and storage on Supabase (Ireland), the analysis server on Render (Germany).

Your rights

Use Profile → Download my data to get a copy and Profile → Delete account to delete everything, or email us. We answer within 30 days. Düdük is not directed at children under 13.
